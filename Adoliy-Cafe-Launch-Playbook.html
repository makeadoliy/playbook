Why digital
It all starts

On their phone

People find cafes online before they find them on the street.

Discovery is on Reels

Young customers pick weekend spots from Instagram Reels and food creators. If the cafe isn't there, it's invisible to them.

“Cafe near me” wins walk-ins

Rating, photos and reviews on Google Maps decide the visit. An active profile is the cheapest footfall source there is.

Kerala orders on WhatsApp

Menus, bookings, party orders and offers move fastest on WhatsApp. It's the one list the cafe owns outright.

Consistency builds trust

When logo, colours and tone match everywhere, people remember the cafe after one visit.

Brand consistency
One cafe

Same look, everywhere

These rules are locked in Week 1. Every designer, editor and staff member works from them. Tick off each touchpoint as it goes live.

Logo
Primary logo + a round mark for profile pictures, cup stamps and stickers. Fixed clear space. Never stretched, recoloured or placed on busy photos.
Colour
1 hero colour, 1 accent, 2 neutrals. Exact HEX codes saved in the brand kit file. Offers and prices always use the accent.
Hero
Accent
Light
Dark
Sample palette. Replace with the cafe's own.
Type
1 display font, 1 handwritten accent, 1 body font, plus a matching Malayalam font for Malayalam and Manglish posts.

Photos
Same light, same angles, same edit. Warm window light, three fixed angles (top-down, 45°, hand-in-frame), one Lightroom preset on every image.
Voice
Warm, short, local. Friendly Manglish captions, never corporate.
“Rain outside, chaya inside. Table for two?” “Puthiya cold brew vannu. First sip on us till Sunday.” “We are pleased to announce the launch of our new beverage.”
Grid
A repeating 3-post rhythm so the profile reads as one brand at a glance.
